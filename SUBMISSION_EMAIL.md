Please find attached my Master's thesis for assessment:

**GPT-NL: Complexity-Aware Energy Estimation of the Data Curation Pipeline via Extended Kalman Filtering**
MSc Applied Data Science, Utrecht University, July 2026.

The thesis was carried out within the GPT-NL project under the supervision of Julio de Oliveira Filho (TNO), Martino Mensio (TNO), and Lisa Bylinina (Utrecht University). It builds and validates a pre-run energy predictor for the GPT-NL data curation pipeline on Snellius: small calibration runs fit a per-stage linear energy law, an Extended Kalman Filter refines the forecast during the live run, and a transfer model extends the prediction to corpora that were never measured.

The required **AI Disclosure statement** is included in the thesis, directly after the title pages (page iii). In short: generative AI (Claude, Anthropic) was used for LaTeX structuring, language editing of my drafts, and code refactoring; it was not used for experiment design, measurement, analysis, or conclusions, and every reported number is reproducible from the published analysis scripts and measurement data.
